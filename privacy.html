Codex Backup Privacy Policy

Codex Backup is a personal utility for its owner. The utility runs on the owner's Mac and uses rclone to connect to Google Drive.

Google Drive access

The Google permission grants read and write access to Drive files. The utility uses that permission to locate the existing backup folder, inspect its backup files and checksums, upload backup files, and download them for recovery or verification. It also checks the signed-in account during setup.

Storage and use

Google sign-in credentials issued to the utility are stored locally on the owner's Mac. Backup files remain in the owner's private storage. The utility uses Drive data for backup, verification, and recovery. It does not use Drive data for advertising or model training, sell it, or share it with other people.

Retention and removal

The owner controls how long to keep backup files and can remove them in Google Drive. The owner can revoke the utility's Google access from their Google Account and remove its local connection settings. Revoking access leaves existing backup files in Drive.

Questions can go to the support contact shown on the Google consent screen.
